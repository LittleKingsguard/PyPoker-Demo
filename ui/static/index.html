How to play

The table

Each league member carries the game it was trained on. The shipped R1 checkpoints play heads-up fixed-limit: 20 chips each, blinds 1/2, bets of 2 before the flop and on it and 4 on the turn and river, with four raises per street. The probe checkpoints play six-seat no-limit: 100 chips each, blinds 1/2, and any bet from the minimum raise up to all-in. Baselines play whichever table you choose.

The league

Checkpoints play greedily with exploration off. The six deterministic policies are fixed rules and never learn. A mixed six-seat table keeps your chosen member and fills the other seats from the league.

This is a demonstration table, not a claim of poker skill. Model cards are revealed only at showdown; a folded hand stays mucked. A checkpoint whose external training artefact is missing is shown as unavailable rather than played.
