# training — one episode into transitions

- **Layer:** L3 · **Module:** `training` · **Unit:** phase1 · **Rev:** 5 · **Date:** 2026-11-10
- **Owner:** how one `engine.play_hand` episode becomes `records.md` §4 transitions for the learner's seat — the seat count, the rotation and the one place a hand is seated (§1), the assembled policy and the vocabulary it decodes in (§2), the decision ordering, the index and the run's own chips in play (§3), `reward` and `next_legal` (§4), and the one batch per hand handed to `agents.learn` (§5); **Rev 2** counts the five granted `agents` calls, `learners()` included (D-19); **Rev 3** states that this step is the one place a utility is applied and hands its arithmetic to `reward.md` (D-20); **Rev 4** threads the run's own **shape** through the policy and the transition step, so the decode, the conservation guard and the shortfall normaliser read that shape's chips in play (`shapes.md` §2–§3, `sizing.md` §5, D-29); **Rev 5** reads the seat count from `engine.game_config` and moves the seating into `_hand`, so a run rotates the learner through its own shape's seats and a shape with more than two seats is dealt `seats − 1` distinct opponents (`seats.md` §2–§5, `shapes.md` §8, ASM-42, ASM-43, ASM-44, D-33)

## 1. The learner's seat: `_seats`, `_seat` and `_hand`

    _seats(shape=None) -> int                                    # the shape's seat count, from engine.game_config(shape)
    _seat(index, shape=None) -> int                              # index mod seats: the seat the learner plays in hand `index`
    _hand(pool, shape, seed, index, policy) -> (played, seat)     # hand `index` of the run at `seed`, and the seat held in it

`_seat(i, shape)` is `i mod _seats(shape)`, and `_seats` is `engine.game_config(shape)["seats"]` — the one place a hand's seat count is declared, which is why neither this module nor a caller of `_seat` writes a `2` or a `6` down as the game's (`seats.md` §2–§3, `engine/surface.md` §3, ASM-44, D-29). On a two-seat shape the rotation is the `i % 2` a run has always had, so no default-shape hand moves, and on a shape with more seats it is each seat held for one hand in `seats` (`shapes.md` §5, `seats.md` §4, ASM-43, D-33).

Seat 0 holds the button and posts the small blind in **every** hand (`engine/surface.md` §4), so rotating the seat the learner plays is how the run rotates it across positions: with two seats that is half its hands on the button and half in the big blind, the same seat alternation a table cell makes and the same fairness the league reports, and with six it is one hand in six in each of six positions — because position is a real and unequal advantage in a multi-way pot, and a fixed seat would make a seed's chips a measurement of the position rather than of the policy (`league.md` §3, `seats.md` §4, D-16, D-22). The index is the hand's 0-based index in that pair's pass, so the same index names the same seat for both arms of a pair, for every rung at one seed, and for the greedy pass that replays the pair's own hands (`seeds.md` §3, `greedy.md` §1).

**One hand, one place.** `_hand` is the loop's and the pass's single way of dealing hand *i*: it derives that hand's deal seed and opponent draw seed from `(seed, i)`, takes the seat `_seat(index, shape)`, fills the remaining seats, and returns `(played, seat)` — the episode `engine` returned and the seat the learner held in it. Because training and the greedy pass both call it, they cannot drift into two samples (`seeds.md` §2, ASM-2, `loop.md` §1 step 5, `greedy.md` §1). A **two-seat** shape is played exactly as every run has played it: one opponent from `evaluation.opponent(pool, draw_seed)`, and the two positional policies swapped hand by hand because seat 0 holds the button — `engine.play_hand(hand_seed, policy, opponent, shape=shape)` where the learner holds seat 0, the two swapped where it holds seat 1 (`e2e-surface.md` §3.1, `engine/surface.md` §4, `shapes.md` §5). A shape with **more seats** draws `seats − 1` distinct opponents in one call — `evaluation.opponent(pool, draw_seed, seats - 1)`, drawn without replacement — inserts the learner's policy at `seat`, and plays the hand through `engine.play_hand(hand_seed, seats=tuple(seating), shape=shape)`, so no seat faces itself and no policy is seated twice (`e2e-surface.md` §3.1, §3.4, `seats.md` §4, ASM-43, D-33). A pool holding fewer members than the draw is `evaluation`'s `DrawExceedsMembership`, propagated unchanged in its owner's class, because a refusal re-wrapped here would hide which module owns the contract (`records.md` §8, `module-map.md` §2, `seats.md` §4).

Rejected: drawing the seat, which makes a run's seat assignment a second stream; a fixed seat, which hands one arm the button throughout and makes a curve a positional artefact (`protocol.md` §1); alternating inside a hand, which is the engine's business and no caller's (`engine/surface.md` §4); and either loop seating the hand itself, which is two seatings that can drift into two samples (`greedy.md` §1, ASM-43).

## 2. The learner's side is a policy assembled here: `_learner_policy`

    _learner_policy(learner, encoder, shape=None) -> callable     # a policy in the sense of e2e-surface.md §2

The returned callable takes `(readout, legal_actions)` and answers one descriptor of `legal_actions`, built from the four granted calls and nothing else:

    mask  = features.legal_indices(legal_actions)        # the decision's legal indices, position-aligned
    obs   = features.encode(encoder, readout)            # one observation for this readout
    index = agents.act(learner, obs, mask)["action"]     # the training-time decision
    return features.decode_action(index, shape=shape)    # the descriptor of the run's own vocabulary

**The index decodes in the run's own shape's vocabulary.** An index is one of its own shape's descriptors and of no other's, so `decode_action` is handed the shape the learner was built on: the `{0, 1, 2}` a run has always used on the default shape, and that shape's own six on either uncapped one (`shapes.md` §2–§3, `sizing.md` §3, `features/surface.md` §9). A closure that decoded with no shape would hand `engine` a default-shape descriptor for a six-index decision, which the engine refuses as an illegal action — so the shape is a parameter of the policy and is never left to default (`engine/surface.md` §10, D-29). The greedy pass's policy is `agents.as_policy` on the same encoder and the same name, so it decides in the same vocabulary (`greedy.md` §1).

This is why `agents.as_policy` is not the training-time decision: the one exploration-off policy this module is granted is the **pass's**, at `exploration="off"` and at the finished model (`greedy.md` §1, `docs/L2/training/surface.md` §1), and the policy above is the training-time decision — `act` at the learner's own seed and its own state, built from the four `features` and `agents` calls this step is granted (`module-map.md` §2, `agents/surface.md` §6, `agents/rule.md` §4). Nothing is recorded here: the policy keeps no log and returns no index, so the transitions are derived from the episode record alone and a unit case can build them without a learner (`tests/training/test_episode.py::TRN-4.1`).

Rejected: `agents.as_policy(learner, encoder, seed)` as the training-time policy, a call outside the grant; catching its absence and falling back, which would be two policies in one loop; decoding with no `shape`, which is the default shape's vocabulary for every run and a descriptor the engine refuses on the other (D-29); and recording the index inside the closure, which would make the batch a side channel of the policy rather than a function of the episode.

## 3. `_transitions(episode, seat, encoder, utility=None, shape=None)`

    _transitions(episode, seat, encoder, utility=None, shape=None) -> list[Mapping]   # records.md §4 records, in decision order

For the decisions where the learner's seat acted, **in the order the hand played them**:

1. `observation` — `features.encode(encoder, decision["readout"])`, the state that seat faced;
2. `legal` — `features.legal_indices(decision["legal"])`, the mask of that decision;
3. `action` — the vocabulary **index** of the descriptor the hand applied: `legal_indices` is position-aligned with its argument (`features/surface.md` §8), so the index is `legal[legal_actions.index(decision["action"])]` and no second legality channel is invented. `agents.learn` takes indices, and an index outside `legal` is its refusal (`agents/surface.md` §7, §2);
4. `next_observation`, `next_legal` — the **next decision of that seat** in the same hand, encoded and masked the same way;
5. `terminal`, `reward` — §4.

The successor is the learner's next decision and never an opponent's interleaved one, and the seat's own decisions stay the seat's however many seats the hand holds: the chain is the seat's own states, so the update never bootstraps on a value computed for another seat's decision (`agents/rule.md` §2, `seats.md` §4). Rejected: taking every decision of the hand and marking one seat's, which would put five opponents' states in the learner's successor on a six-seat hand and make the update depend on who happened to act next.

**`shape` is the run's own, and it reaches every arithmetic that reads the game.** The three reward functions take it: `_chips_in_play(decision, seed, seat, shape=None)` totals `sum(readout['stacks']) + readout['pot']` and compares it with `engine.game_config(shape)`'s seats × starting stack, `_stack_shortfall(decision, seed, seat, shape=None)` normalises against that same starting stack, and `_net_chips(decision, seed, seat, shape=None)` reads the decision's own reward and needs none of it (`utilities.md` §4, `shapes.md` §2, `reward.md` §4, D-20, D-29). So a run conserves **40 chips on the default shape, 200 on `no-limit-100` and 600 on `six-seat-no-limit-100`** — each shape's seats × its starting stack — and `utilities.md` §2's ±2-chip and all-in scores are that shape's own arithmetic (`seats.md` §2, ASM-42, D-33). **A readout of one shape read against another is `ChipConservationBroken`**, naming the hand's seed and both totals: a 200-chip decision read against the default's 40, a 600-chip one against 200, or a 40-chip one against 600 is refused rather than normalised, because a normaliser that is not the game's own would make one reward incommensurable with the next (ASM-6, ASM-28, `errors.md` §2, `loop.md` §6, D-29).

**A hand in which the learner's seat never acted yields an empty list.** That is a real hand — an opponent may fold preflop before the learner acts, and on a six-seat table most seats fold before the button in many hands — and it is not an error: the run makes no `learn` call, the hand contributes no signal, and `loop.md` §1 step 6 says so (`agents/surface.md` §7 refuses an empty batch, and nothing here asks it to; `seats.md` §4).

## 4. `terminal`, `reward`, and the terminal transition's own mask

- **`terminal` is positional**: the last transition of the list — the last decision that seat made in the hand — carries `terminal=True`, and every earlier one carries `False`. The seat's last decision is its terminal one even when the hand ended afterwards in another seat, because the hand's outcome arrives at the seat's own terminal decision (`records.md` §5, ASM-12, `seats.md` §4).
- **`reward` is the decision's own at every decision but the terminal one**: those keep the episode's `0.0` — the engine places the hand's delta at the seat's terminal decision alone, and no intermediate term is added (`records.md` §5, ASM-12). At the terminal decision the reward is re-expressed by the variant's **utility**: **this is the one place a utility is applied**, and `reward.md` §1–§4 owns the arithmetic, the selection of it by the catalogue's reward token and the chips-in-play guard (D-20, `utilities.md` §4).
- **No reward arithmetic in `src/agents/learner.py` is touched by any of it**: its terminal branch stays `target = record["reward"]` and its non-terminal branch stays the rule's, so the utility never enters the update — the learner carries the utility's name and reads no arithmetic from it (`utilities.md` §3, §4, D-19, D-20).
- **The terminal transition's `next_observation` and `next_legal` are its own**: it has no successor and `agents.learn` reads neither, and an empty mask is illegal, so the mask it carries is the mask of the decision it is (`features/surface.md` §8, `agents/rule.md` §3, `tests/e2e/_surface.py` `transitions`).
- **A zero-reward terminal decision is normal**: a split pot pays nothing, so `reward == 0.0` at a terminal transition is not a sign the flag is wrong — the flag is positional and never inferred from the reward (§4's first bullet).

## 5. The batch, and what a hand costs the learner

`agents.learn(learner, transitions)` is called **once per hand** with that hand's list, in the order §3 built it: the caller's order, neither sampled nor shuffled nor replayed (`loop.md` §1 step 6, `agents/surface.md` §7, ASM-7). That is what lets two arms declare one sample order (`ladder.md` §5). One hand holds a handful of decisions — the fixed-limit cap bounds it at about eight applied actions per street, and the game's own ceiling at 23 a hand on the default shape (`ASM-6`, `ASM-15`, `sizing.md` §4) — so a hand's batch is **smaller than the declared `batch_size` of 32**, and that is legal by construction: `batch_size` is the ladder's declared granularity and never a precondition, and a rule stated per transition consumes any non-empty batch in the caller's order (`agents/rule.md` §3, §6). A shape whose history capacity is 66 or 98 admits hands the default shape's 24 slots refuse, so the cap above is the default shape's and no wider claim is made here (`shapes.md` §8, ASM-42, D-33).

The batch's `td_error` — the mean absolute TD error over the hand's transitions (`agents/surface.md` §7) — is the hand's learning signal and is filed into the run's current interval (`curves.md` §3). Nothing here keeps a replay buffer, a priority or a second pass, and no transition survives its hand.

## 6. What is left to L4

Whether the descriptor's position is found by `list.index` or by an enumerate over the pairs, whether the observations are computed once and reused for the successor, how `shape` reaches the three reward functions, and the internals of those functions. Not L4's: `_seat`'s `index mod seats` rotation and that the seat count is `engine.game_config`'s, `_hand`'s one seating of a hand — the two positional policies on a two-seat shape, one counted draw and `play_hand`'s `seats` sequence on a shape with more — and the learner's seat being `_seat`'s; the four calls of §2 and the descriptor round trip, the index decoding in the run's own vocabulary, the successor rule of §3, the terminal flag's positional definition, the terminal reward being the utility's number and `0.0` at every other decision, the guard and the normaliser reading the run's own chips in play and refusing another shape's readout by name, the terminal mask, and the empty-batch rule (`reward.md` §1–§5, `guardrails.md` G7, G10, G17, G25, `seats.md` §4, ASM-43, D-29). The three signatures of §1 and §3 — `_seats`' and `_seat`'s `shape`, `_hand`'s five arguments and its `(played, seat)` pair, and `_transitions`' `utility` and `shape` — are contract for `tests/training/` (`README.md` §4).
