## 1. Purpose, and what is real

The deck opens on the speaker's contact and closes on it: scan at the open, scan at the close. `#s2` sits between the title and the talk's first content slide; the outtro — **`#s9` since D-133 deleted the five demo slides that sat between them, `#s14` before that, and `#s8` when this document was written** — **replaced** the demo closing slide, whose placeholder copy was deleted rather than kept beside real content: D-129 forbids a slide that is half real and half placeholder, and placeholder copy on a card carrying a real address is exactly that half.

**`#s2` and `#s9` are two of the deck's nine real slides** — the other seven are the title (`#s1`, D-131) and the six content slides D-132 added (`#s3`–`#s8`, `content.md`). **All nine are real now:** D-133 deleted the five demo slides (which sat at `#s9`–`#s13` until it did) and their `DEMO` badges with them, so no slide carries a badge and nothing on the wall is placeholder (A4, `design.md` §5.3). `content-rules.md` therefore binds on both contact slides, as it does on the other seven: the contact block carries no figure, so it owes no `FACTCHECK.md` row, and nothing on either slide may become a measurement without one. The felt, the cards, the stage, the type ramp and the navigation do not change.

## 2. The QR block: the drawing contract

**One inline `<svg class="qr">` per contact slide**, drawn from the run-length rows — not an image, not a font, not a `<canvas>`, not a data URI, and never generated in JS (the file must read correctly with the script off).

    <svg class="qr" id="qr2" viewBox="0 0 37 37" width="296" height="296"
         role="img" aria-labelledby="qr2-t" shape-rendering="crispEdges">
      <title id="qr2-t">LinkedIn profile QR code — Ryan Rasey</title>
      <rect x="4" y="4" width="7" height="1"/>      … one per dark run, 212 of them
    </svg>

**Geometry.** `viewBox="0 0 37 37"`: 29 modules plus a **4-module quiet zone on every side** (4 + 29 + 4 = 37). One `<rect>` per dark run, all coordinates integer **viewBox units**, never px: `x = startCol + 4`, `y = row + 4`, `width = runLength`, `height = 1`. Nothing is drawn in the quiet zone — no background rect, no frame, no `<use>`, no `<defs>`, no `<image>`, no `<style>`, no `transform`, no gradient — so the quiet zone **is the ivory card face**, and nothing else is painted there.
**Size.** `width="296" height="296"` — 37 units at exactly **8 px per module** (29 × 8 = 232 px of modules, 232 + 2 × 32 = 296 px with the quiet zone), so every module edge lands on a whole design px. On the stage that is 8 px/module; at 1920×1080 the stage is exactly ×1.5, so **12 px/module, 444 px** for the block. The module size is an **even integer** by rule, which is what keeps ×1.5, ×2 and ×1 crisp. **Minimum: 6 design px per module (9 px at 1080p).** Both slides in this pass draw **8** (12), and a later placement may go to 6 but never below it — below that the code is a texture, not a code.
**Paint.** No `fill` attribute on any rect: one rule in the deck's `<style>`, `#s2 .qr rect, #s9 .qr rect { fill: var(--ink) }` — `--ink` on `--card-face`, 13.4:1, dark on light. **Never inverted, never a theme accent, no opacity, no filter**: contrast and quiet zone are what make it scannable, so neither is a styling choice. The block itself carries **no border, radius, background, shadow, outline or opacity**, and none of the five cards on the two contact slides carries a **suit glyph or a pip** — a corner glyph's box lands inside the quiet zone's territory — while a hairline `card__rule` is allowed **above** the block, never inside it (`#s9` has one there, §3).
**Accessibility.** The QR is content, so it is named: `role="img"` plus a `<title>` reading `LinkedIn profile QR code — Ryan Rasey`, referenced by `aria-labelledby` (ids `qr2-t` / `qr8-t`, unique in the document). It is **never `aria-hidden`**, and it is not a link. The printed URL beside it is what serves a reader who cannot scan, which is why §3 sets it in full (§4).

**`.t-url` has one form on both slides:** a 56px box of two `<span>` lines, `display: block`, each 28px, `--font-mono` at `--fs-small`; line 1 `https://www.linkedin.com/`, line 2 `in/ryan-rasey-930403a4/`. 24 characters a line at ≈12.5px per mono character = **300 px**, inside the 336px card and comfortably inside the 720px one — so the break is a wrap of one unbroken string at a path boundary, not a shortening: every character of the URL is on the slide, and C4 checks the concatenation. Both lines are `white-space: nowrap`.
**Character budgets** (design §5's method, ≈18px per character at 28px, scaled): “Founder/Dev @ Auspicion” at 28px ≈ 396 ≤ 720; the email at 28px ≈ 432 ≤ 720 and at 20px ≈ 310 ≤ 336; the URL lines ≈ 300 ≤ 336; “Scan to connect” ≈ 194 ≤ 336; “Ryan Rasey” at 56px ≈ 358 ≤ 720 and at 40px ≈ 256 ≤ 336.
**The clip rules these boxes need, and only these.** All the new boxes are direct children of `.card`, so `html.js .card > *:not(.suit)` already clips them; the two `.t-url` line spans are grandchildren and need their own: `html.js #s2 .a-wide .t-url span` and `html.js #s9 .a-side-bottom .t-url span` — `overflow: hidden; white-space: nowrap`. Add one `#sN`-scoped clip rule per new fixed-height box anyway, as the README's step 3 requires: `#s2 .a-wide` `.t-h1`/`.t-h3`/`.t-email`/`.t-url`, `#s2 .a-side` `.qr`/`.t-note`, `#s9 .a-wide` `.t-h1`/`.qr`/`.t-note`, `#s9 .a-side-top` `.t-h2`/`.t-note`, `#s9 .a-side-bottom` `.t-h3`/`.t-url` (for `.qr` the clip is a no-op — the box is exactly its viewBox — but it is declared so the shape is uniform). **No `nowrap` on the text boxes**: the URL is already split by `span`, and a nowrap headline would clip silently instead of wrapping.

## 6. Acceptance checks

**C1 (gate).** `grep -c 'http'` = 0; no `url(` in the deck's own CSS, `@import`, `@font-face`, `<link`, `xmlns`; exactly one `<style>` and one `<script>`, neither with `src`/`href`; `grep -c 'innerHTML'` = 0. **`<img` and `src=` are no longer banned here** — D-132 replaced that ban with A2's relative-only rule and G12's check, and a grep that stays green for the wrong reason is worse than no grep (`checks.md` §3.2). **C2 (gate).** Nine `section.slide`; `id` `s1`…`s9` in file order; `data-shape` in `content.md` §1's order, nine distinct values; nine `.dot` with exactly one `.is-on` on the active index. **C3 (gate).** `grep -c 'class="demo-badge"'` = 0 and `grep -c 'demo-note'` = 0 — D-133 deleted the sampler and its badge, so this check now proves the absence rather than counting placeholder slides, and the HUD carries no `placeholder content`. **C4 (gate).** The rendered `#s2 .t-url` and `#s9 .a-side-bottom .t-url` `textContent` — the two spans' strings concatenated — equals `https://www.linkedin.com/in/ryan-rasey-930403a4/` character for character, while the file's own text carries no `http`: the URL is written with its scheme's first letter as a character reference (`&#104;ttps://www.linkedin.com/`), which renders as the full address and keeps A2's grep at zero. A URL shortener, a bare `linkedin.com` or a dropped scheme fails this check.
**C5 (gate).** Two `svg.qr` in the file, one per contact slide: `viewBox="0 0 37 37"`, `width`/`height` `296`, `role="img"`, one `<title>` naming the profile, `aria-labelledby` pointing at it, **not** `aria-hidden`, no `xmlns`, `shape-rendering="crispEdges"`, and no `href`, `xlink`, `<use>`, `<defs>`, `<image>`, `<style>`, `transform` or `fill` attribute inside. **C6 (gate).** Each `svg.qr` holds exactly **212** `<rect>` elements (424 in the file) with integer `x`, `y`, `width` and `height = 1`, and **no dark geometry within 4 modules of the block's edge**: `4 ≤ x`, `x + width ≤ 33`, `4 ≤ y ≤ 32`. The 29 rows show as exactly 29 distinct `y` values. **C7 (gate).** Each `svg.qr` renders 296×296 design px (444×444 device px at ×1.5, 12 device px per module), `display: block`, inside its card, `fill` the `--ink` token at 13.4:1, and nothing is painted in its quiet ring — no card glyph, rule, border, shadow or background inside the block, card face only.
**C8 (reviewer-only, and the one that decides readiness).** Screenshot `#/2` and `#/9` with `firefox --headless --no-remote --profile <dir> --screenshot OUT.png --window-size=1280,720 "file://<abs>/presentation/index.html#/2"` (repeat at `--window-size=1920,1080`, the projector's case), crop the `.qr` block out of the PNG, sample each of the 29×29 module centres to a matrix, and **decode it: the string must equal the URL in C4.** A verified source matrix drawn wrongly is still a dead QR on the wall (D-129), so no gate-clean run and no reviewer's glance replaces this step.
**Which is which.** C1–C7 are mechanical and belong to the gate: `checks.md` G1 carries C1 and C3's greps, G3 carries C2, and the new **G11** carries C5–C7 (all DOM assertions, no sixth BiDi command). **C8 is reviewer-only** — decoding needs a QR decoder, none is installed (D-129 rejected generating with a library), so the gate stops at C6's geometry and its screenshots are the artefacts the reviewer decodes. A clean gate is still not a design review (`checks.md` §5): whether the code is big enough to scan from the back of the room is a look at the render, and C8 at 1920×1080 is the honest form of that question.
